## How the counts were made

Keywords are the ones the app read from each ad. A keyword is "on your base CV" when it appears in either base
CV (English or Danish) as written, and "backed" when the same phrase (a name exactly, other words by their stem)
appears in your career database. Both are word matches, so the verdicts above were checked by hand against the
database. Synonyms the ads spell differently were grouped where it mattered: troubleshooting, Azure, Agile, OOP.

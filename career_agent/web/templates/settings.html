Claude through Claude Code

Uses the Claude subscription you're logged in with (Pro or Max) instead of an API key, so there's no bill per call: it counts against your plan's usage limits, and when you hit one, tasks wait and try again later. Job sources

Watched automatically. New jobs are screened, read and scored; strong matches get a draft. For Jobindex, run a search on jobindex.dk and paste the address of the results page (it becomes an RSS feed automatically). Most job sites' search pages work, as do any RSS feeds. The app follows each site's rules for robots (robots.txt) and tells you when an address isn't allowed. On Jobindex that means up to two search words and a region (area=storkbh), but no distance, municipality or page number, so a feed shows the newest 20 jobs. To get every job in a filtered search, or Jobnet, collect the results pages yourself (below).

Collect jobs from a page you have open

A job board's filtered search (category, distance, page 2 and on) is closed to robots, so the app can't watch it. You can open it yourself and hand the page to the app. The app fetches nothing from the site: it reads the jobs that were on your screen, drops the ones it already has, and screens the rest like any other source.

Collect jobs ← drag this button to your browser's Favourites bar (Safari: View → Show Favourites Bar).

  1. Keep Career Agent running.
  2. Run your search on the job site, with any filters.
  3. Click Collect jobs in the Favourites bar. One click takes the whole search: on Jobindex and IT-Jobbank it reads every page of results (up to 50, a little apart), and on Jobnet it presses “Indlæs flere job” until all the jobs are showing. A box on the page says how far it has got.
  4. Press Send to Career Agent in the box. A tab opens, says how many jobs it took, and closes again. (A search with just one page is sent at once.)

A zip with your database, your files and exported PDFs, copied to a folder you choose. A folder in Nextcloud puts it on your server as well.

Collect bookmark jobs

Your {{ searches|length }} saved search{{ 'es' if searches|length != 1 }} are sites the app isn't allowed to check by itself, so you open each one and hand it over with the Collect jobs bookmark. It takes about a minute per search.

Once: put the bookmark in your browser

  1. Show the bookmarks bar. Firefox: View → Toolbars → Bookmarks Toolbar → Always Show (or right-click the empty area next to the tabs). Safari: View → Show Favourites Bar. Chrome or Edge: ⌘⇧B.
  2. Drag this button up onto the bar: Collect jobs

Already there? Skip this. Made it before 19 November 2026? Delete the old one and drag it again.

Each time

  1. Press the button below. The search opens in a new tab.
  2. When the results show, click Collect jobs in the bookmarks bar. A box in the bottom-right corner counts the pages it reads.
  3. Press Send to Career Agent in that box. A Career Agent tab opens and says how many jobs were new.
  4. In that tab, press Next: … and repeat from step 2, until it says that was the last one. The new jobs are screened while you go.
